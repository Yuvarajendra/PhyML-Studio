Physics-informed machine learning

        
From published research to a working tool: learn the method, bring your problem, and put physics-informed AI to use.

      

      

        What we do
        
Research code, made into workbenches

        
PhyML Studio turns open-source code from leading research on physics-informed neural networks into interactive workbenches for fluid mechanics, solid mechanics, casting, manufacturing and related fields.

        
Each workbench runs on a reference dataset from the start, and accepts your own data from CAD, ANSYS Fluent and CFX, Siemens NX, COMSOL and OpenFOAM. No coding is needed to learn the method, develop and optimise a model, and use it in your work.
